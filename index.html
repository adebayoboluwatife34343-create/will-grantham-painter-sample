The trades

One painter. Many crafts.

  1. Interior and exterior painting. Colour specialist: matching, mixing and advice on all types of paints, finishes and varnishes.

  2. Renovation, restoration and design, with a specialist knowledge of historical buildings, their construction, decorative techniques and context.

  3. Murals, panoramas, oil painting, gilding, polished Venetian plaster, lime wash, linseed paint, faux bois and marble, and restoration of decorative plasterwork.

  4. Cornices, skirtings, wainscoting, panelling, thresholds, door fitting and decorative mouldings, plus emergency work after a break-in.

  5. Advice, hanging, and hand made wallpapers, including papers made from your own designs.

  6. Tiling, complete bathrooms, showers and kitchens, floors from laying new to restoring old, lighting design and installation, and garden planning and building.

Every line above follows Will's own public Nextdoor description. He confirms the final list.
